PRICAT — PRICE/SALES CATALOGUE (PRICAT)

EDIFACT Message Type
📄

A price catalogue conveying the product list, prices and packaging details in bulk.

Who sends it

Sent by the supplier to the buyer.

PRICAT is normally used for master-data exchange ahead of ordering. The buyer loads the supplier's product and price list into its own system so that orders carry the correct barcode and price.

A line may carry several PRI segments: AAA net, AAB gross and AAE recommended retail price. Country of origin comes from ALI and the pack quantity from PAC.

Typical Segment Flow

UNB → UNH → BGM → DTM → NAD → CUX → LIN → IMD → PRI → PAC → UNT → UNZ
