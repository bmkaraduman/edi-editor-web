RFF — Reference Information

EDIFACT segment
📘

References another document. ON is the order number, DQ the despatch advice number, IV the invoice number and CT the contract number.

Example

RFF+ON:PO-2026-0042'

Data Elements

Pos.Description
01Reference Type:Value

Watch out

The link between an invoice and its order is normally made with RFF+ON; without it, automatic matching is impossible.
