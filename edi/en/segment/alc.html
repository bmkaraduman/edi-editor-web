ALC — Allowance or Charge / Discount

EDIFACT segment
📘

Declares an allowance or a charge. A means allowance (discount), C means charge.

Example

ALC+A++++DI::ADET ISKONTOSU'

Data Elements

No element descriptions are defined for this segment.

Watch out

ALC is normally read together with the PCD (percentage) or MOA (amount) segment that follows; on its own it carries no value.
