TAX — Duty / Tax Details

EDIFACT segment
📘

Carries tax information: the type of tax (such as VAT) and its rate.

Example

TAX+7+VAT+++:::20'

Data Elements

No element descriptions are defined for this segment.

Code List

CodeMeaning
6Tax Exempt (No VAT)
7Tax
ENVEnvironmental Tax
FUNC_7Tax Applicable
GSTGST
TYPE_VATVAT
VATVAT

Watch out

The rate normally sits in the last sub-element and is expressed as a percentage, so 20 means 20%.
