PRI — Price Information

EDIFACT segment
📘

Carries a unit price. AAA is the net price, AAB the gross price and AAE the recommended retail price.

Example

PRI+AAA:1.85'

Data Elements

Pos.Description
01Price Detail (Type:Amount)

Code List

CodeMeaning
AAANet Price
AABGross Price
AAERecommended Retail Price (Shelf)

Watch out

PRI does not carry a currency; the currency comes from the CUX segment at message level. This split is easy to overlook.
