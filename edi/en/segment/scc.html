SCC — Scheduling / Stock Status

EDIFACT segment
📘

States the nature of a delivery schedule: a firm order, a forecast, or a planning estimate. It is the decisive field in delivery schedule messages.

Example

SCC+1'

Data Elements

No element descriptions are defined for this segment.

Code List

CodeMeaning
1Firm
4Forecast

Watch out

This code determines commercial commitment. Treating a quantity marked as a forecast as though it were a firm order, and starting production on it, is one of the most expensive mistakes a supplier can make.
