GID — Goods Detail (Seq No)

EDIFACT segment
📘

Opens a goods item in a transport instruction. It states how many packages it consists of and their type; the PAC, MEA and HAN segments that follow belong to this item.

Example

GID+1+24:CT'

Data Elements

No element descriptions are defined for this segment.

Code List

CodeMeaning
CTCarton / Box
PXPallet

Watch out

The first element is the item sequence and has nothing to do with a product code. What matters to the carrier is how many cartons travel; product-level detail is not made here, it lives in the order and the despatch advice.
