BGM — Beginning of Message / Doc Type

EDIFACT segment
📘

Identifies the document itself: its type (order, invoice, despatch advice...), its number, and the purpose of the message (original, cancellation, change).

Example

BGM+220+PO-2026-0042+9'

Watch out

The third element matters: 9 means original, 1 cancellation, 4 change. A wrong value here can create duplicate orders downstream.
