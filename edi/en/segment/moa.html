MOA — Monetary Amount

EDIFACT segment
📘

States a monetary amount. 203 is the line amount, 79 the goods total, 124 the tax amount and 77 the invoice grand total.

Example

MOA+203:888.00'

Data Elements

Watch out

A message contains several MOA segments, each meaning a different total. Summing them without checking the qualifier produces wrong figures.
