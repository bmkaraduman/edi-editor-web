Buying gifts for gamers can feel risky: they know their gear inside out, and a wrong guess ends up in a drawer. The good news is that many of the best gifts for gamers are not games at all. This guide covers accessories, setup and comfort upgrades, board games and merch, plus a few tricks to get it right.

## How to avoid buying a game they already own

Games are the obvious gift, but they are also the easiest to get wrong. Many gamers buy new releases on day one, and digital libraries are invisible to everyone else. A few ways around it:

- **Ask what platform they play on**: PC, a specific console or a handheld. A game or accessory for the wrong system is useless.
- **Check their wish list**, which many digital game stores let users share with friends.
- **Ask a friend they play with**, who usually knows exactly what they are waiting for.
- **Give a digital gift card for their platform's store**, a safe choice that lets them pick the game themselves.

If you would rather give something physical, skip the games and focus on the gear and comfort ideas below.

## Gaming accessories

Accessories are a great middle ground: useful, personal and easy to upgrade. Some of the most appreciated:

- **A good gaming headset**, with clear sound and a mic for playing online with friends.
- **An extra controller**, so friends can join in, or a spare when the old one starts to drift.
- **A gaming mouse and large mouse pad**, a big upgrade for PC gamers who still use a basic mouse.
- **A mechanical keyboard**, which many PC gamers love for its feel and sound.
- **A charging dock for controllers**, so they never start a session with a dead battery.
- **A carrying case for a handheld console**, for gamers who play on the go.

Always check compatibility with their console or PC before you buy.

## Setup and comfort upgrades

Gamers spend hours in the same spot, so anything that makes that spot more comfortable is welcome:

- **An ergonomic gaming chair or a seat cushion**, for long sessions without a sore back.
- **LED light strips or a smart lamp**, which make the setup look great and reduce eye strain in a dark room.
- **A headphone stand**, which keeps the desk tidy and the headset safe.
- **A monitor arm or stand**, to free up desk space and set the screen at the right height.
- **A cable management kit**, an unexciting gift that every gamer with a tangle of wires secretly wants.
- **A cozy blanket hoodie or a heated throw**, for long winter evenings of gaming on the sofa.

## Board games for non-video gamers

Not every gamer plays on a screen. Board game fans, and video gamers who like a game night with friends, love these:

- **A strategy board game**, the kind that keeps a group busy for a whole evening.
- **A cooperative game**, where everyone plays together against the game, perfect for mixed groups.
- **A party card game**, quick to learn and good for a laugh with friends and family.
- **A tabletop role-playing starter set**, for gamers who want to tell stories with friends.
- **A puzzle or escape room game in a box**, for people who love solving riddles.
- **A board game storage organizer or a set of nice dice**, for the person who already owns a full shelf.

Board games also make great gifts for families and couples; our [Christmas gift ideas](blog:christmas-gift-ideas) have more game-night inspiration.

## Merch and fun extras

Merch is a great way to show you know what they love. Find out their favorite game or character first:

- **A T-shirt or hoodie** from their favorite game series.
- **Collectible figures**, for the shelf behind the monitor.
- **A mug or water bottle** with a game logo, for daily use at the desk.
- **An art book or a soundtrack on vinyl**, for gamers who love the stories and music behind the games.
- **A themed poster or a framed print**, to decorate the gaming room.

## Gifts for gamers by age and budget

Gamers come in every age, and so do the gifts. Teenagers often want gear for online play, which our guide to [gifts for teenagers](blog:gifts-for-teenagers) covers in more detail, while adult gamers often appreciate comfort upgrades and collector items. For a partner or brother, our [birthday gifts for him](blog:birthday-gifts-for-him) guide also has a section on gamers.

A rough guide to budgets:

- **Under $25:** a mug, a T-shirt, a party card game, a cable management kit or a mouse pad.
- **$25 to $50:** a controller charging dock, LED light strips, a board game or a collectible figure.
- **$50 to $100:** a gaming headset, an extra controller, a mechanical keyboard or a monitor arm.
- **Over $100:** a gaming chair, a premium headset or a group gift toward a new console.

## Final tips

The best gifts for gamers come from listening. Pay attention to what they complain about, like a broken headset or a sore back, and what they are excited about, like an upcoming release. Then pick something that fits their platform and their style of play.
