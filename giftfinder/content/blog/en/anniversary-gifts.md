An anniversary gift does not have to be expensive, but it should say "I remember us." These anniversary gift ideas cover the first year, the traditional themes for each year, big milestones and gifts you can enjoy together, so you can find something that fits your story.

## How to pick an anniversary gift

Before you shop, think about what your partner talks about, what you did together this past year and what you would like to do next. The best anniversary gifts usually do one of three things:

- **Celebrate a shared memory**, like a print of the place you met or a photo book of the year.
- **Create a new memory**, like a weekend away or a class you take together.
- **Show you listen**, like the thing they mentioned months ago and forgot about.

## First anniversary gifts

The first anniversary, whether you are celebrating a year of dating or of marriage, is about looking back on a big year. The traditional theme is paper, which makes for thoughtful, personal gifts:

- **A custom star map or print of a meaningful date and place**, a simple way to frame the night it all started.
- **A photo book of your first year**, which takes some effort and is exactly why it means so much.
- **A love letter or a "reasons I love you" jar**, nearly free and often the gift people keep longest.
- **Concert, theater or game tickets**, also paper, and a promise of a fun night out.
- **A journal for two** with prompts you can fill in together each year.

## Traditional anniversary gifts by year

Many couples like following the traditional anniversary themes, which give each year its own material. They make shopping easier and add a playful challenge:

- **2nd, cotton:** soft bedding, a cozy robe or matching loungewear.
- **3rd, leather:** a wallet, a bag, a watch strap or a leather-bound journal.
- **4th, fruit and flowers:** a fruit tree for the garden, a flower subscription or a fine bottle of wine.
- **5th, wood:** a carved keepsake box, a wooden serving board engraved with your names or a tree you plant together.
- **6th, iron:** a cast-iron skillet for the cook in the house or an iron garden decoration.
- **7th, wool or copper:** a wool blanket for cold nights or copper mugs for a cocktail evening.
- **10th, tin or aluminum:** a vintage-style tin with handwritten notes or a sleek aluminum travel case.

You do not have to stick to the theme literally; a clever twist on the material is often the most memorable part of the gift.

## Milestone anniversaries

Big round numbers call for something a little more special. The classic milestone themes are well known: 15 years is crystal, 20 is china, 25 is silver, 30 is pearl, 40 is ruby and 50 is gold.

- **25th, silver:** a silver necklace, cufflinks or an engraved silver frame with your wedding photo.
- **30th, pearl:** pearl earrings or a pearl pendant, a timeless choice for a timeless couple.
- **40th, ruby:** red-stone jewelry, a ruby-red wine from the year you met or a dinner at a favorite restaurant.
- **50th, gold:** a gold keepsake, a family portrait session or a party with everyone who has been part of the journey.

For parents or grandparents celebrating a milestone, a group gift from the whole family, such as a weekend trip or a memory book with letters from everyone, often means more than any object. See our [gifts for grandparents](blog:gifts-for-grandparents) for more ideas.

## Experience gifts for couples

After a few years together, many couples have all the stuff they need. Time together becomes the most valuable gift:

- **A weekend getaway** to a place you have always talked about, or back to where you went on your first trip.
- **A cooking, wine-tasting or pottery class**, which gives you a new skill and a new story.
- **A hot-air balloon ride or a sunset boat trip** for a once-in-a-lifetime moment.
- **A recreated first date**, the same restaurant, the same movie or the same walk.

## Anniversary gifts by budget and person

You can find a lovely anniversary gift at almost any price:

- **Under $25:** a handwritten letter with flowers, a framed photo, a custom playlist with a printed track list or a favorite treat.
- **$25 to $75:** a personalized print, soft cotton or leather accessories, a couple's game or a nice bottle of wine.
- **$75 to $150:** jewelry, a quality watch, a cooking class or a dinner out.
- **Over $150:** a weekend trip, fine jewelry for a milestone or a bigger shared purchase like a new grill or espresso machine.

## FAQ

### What is the traditional first anniversary gift?

Paper is the traditional first anniversary theme. Think photo books, love letters, custom prints, tickets or a journal you fill in together.

### Should we exchange gifts every anniversary?

There is no rule. Some couples exchange gifts every year, others choose an experience together, and some save gifts for milestone years. Agree on what feels right for both of you so nobody is caught off guard.

### How much should I spend on an anniversary gift?

Spend what feels comfortable. A thoughtful, personal gift under $25 often means more than an expensive one chosen in a hurry, and many couples save bigger budgets for milestones like the 10th or 25th.
