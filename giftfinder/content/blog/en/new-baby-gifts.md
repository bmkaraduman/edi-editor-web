A new baby brings a flood of tiny outfits and teddy bears, and new parents usually end up wishing for something completely different: sleep, hot meals and fewer loads of laundry. The best new baby gifts balance the cute with the useful, and they don't forget the parents or the big brother or sister. Here's what to give, how to size it, and what to skip.

## Before you buy: rules of thumb and budget

1. **Check the registry first.** If the parents made a list, use it. It's there because they've thought about what they need and what they already have.
2. **Size up.** Newborn clothes fit for a few weeks at most, and many babies skip that size entirely. Clothing in 6 to 12 months will be worn when the first wave of gifts no longer fits.
3. **Think about the season.** A winter baby needs warm layers and a snowsuit at the right size in the right months; a summer baby needs light cotton and sun hats.

### How much to spend

- **Under $25**: swaddles, bibs, a board book, a bath set or a big-sibling gift.
- **$25 to $75**: a memory book, a personalized blanket, a diaper caddy with supplies or meal vouchers.
- **$75 to $150**: a baby carrier, a set of clothes for the coming year or a group gift from coworkers.

## Practical newborn gifts parents will use every day

These are rarely glamorous, but parents remember who brought them.

- **Muslin swaddles or wraps**, which work as blankets, burp cloths, nursing covers and sunshades.
- **Zip-up sleepers**, because zippers beat snaps at 3 am.
- **A stack of burp cloths and bibs**, which run out faster than anyone expects.
- **A diaper caddy stocked with diapers in a bigger size, wipes and cream**, a gift that is used up and never wasted.
- **A hooded towel and a soft washcloth set** for bath time.
- **A baby carrier or wrap**, if the parents haven't chosen one yet and you can include a gift receipt.

## Keepsake gifts for the new arrival

For family and close friends who want something lasting.

- **A personalized blanket or wall print** with the baby's name, birth date and birth weight.
- **A baby memory book** where parents can record firsts, from the first smile to the first word.
- **A handprint and footprint kit**, a keepsake that captures how tiny they once were.
- **A first-year photo frame** with slots for each month.
- **A classic children's book with a handwritten message** inside the cover, to read to them now and give back later.

## Gifts for the new parents

New parents rarely get anything for themselves, so this is where you can stand out.

- **Meal delivery vouchers or a batch of home-cooked freezer meals**, maybe the most appreciated gift of all.
- **A large insulated water bottle with a straw** for long feeding sessions.
- **A cozy robe and soft socks for mom**, for the weeks of being at home around the clock.
- **Good coffee, tea or snacks**, energy for the night shifts.
- **An offer of your time**: a written promise to walk the dog, do a grocery run or watch the baby while they nap.

## Don't forget the big sibling

A new baby means a big change for older children, and a small gift of their own helps them feel included.

- **A "big brother" or "big sister" book**, which helps toddlers understand what's happening.
- **A doll with a few baby accessories**, so they can care for "their" baby too.
- **A new puzzle, art set or sticker book**, something to keep them busy while the baby feeds.
- **A T-shirt that says big brother or big sister**, worn with pride on the first visit to the hospital.

## What to avoid

- **Crib bumpers, pillows and loose stuffed toys for the crib**: safe-sleep guidance recommends keeping the crib clear, so many parents won't use them.
- **Strong scents**: perfumed lotions, candles or flowers with a heavy fragrance can bother a newborn and tired parents alike.
- **Toys with small parts** or anything labeled for older children.
- **Too many newborn-size clothes**, which may never be worn.
- **Big gear without asking**: strollers, car seats and cribs are personal choices, and parents usually research them for weeks.

## FAQ

### When should I give a new baby gift?

Before the birth at a baby shower, or in the first weeks after. Visit only when the parents say they're ready, and keep it short. Dropping a gift at the door is always fine.

### How much should I spend on a new baby gift?

It depends on how close you are. Coworkers and acquaintances often give something under $25 or join a group gift, while close friends and family usually spend more.

### Is it okay to give cash or a gift card?

Yes. Many new parents welcome cash, a savings contribution for the child or a voucher for a baby store, especially if they already have the basics. Add a small physical gift or card so it doesn't feel impersonal.
